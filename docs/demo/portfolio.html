Method

  • Started 12 January 2026 with a fixed starting balance held in Worker config, not in the database.
  • Whole shares only. No fractional positions, no leverage, no shorting.
  • Prices are PSX snapshots written to KV every 15 minutes during session hours, not live ticks. A trade prices at the stored snapshot price and stores that as_of.
  • Benchmark is the KSE-100 daily series. The portfolio tracks PSX instruments only.
